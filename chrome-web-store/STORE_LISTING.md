# Acxxel GeM Desktop Workflow

## Summary

Transfers approved Acxxel Desktop catalogue data into GeM forms for authorized Bid Analysers.

## Detailed description

Acxxel GeM Desktop Workflow assists authorized procurement staff with creating
Desktop catalogue offerings on the Government e-Marketplace.

The extension:

- opens the GeM login page after an authorized Acxxel job is started;
- leaves username, password, captcha and OTP entry to the user;
- detects Add New Offering and its multi-step catalogue forms;
- fills approved product specifications, quantity and pricing data;
- attaches approved documents and product images;
- flags missing or ambiguous fields for manual review;
- leaves Next, Save/Proceed and final Submit under user control.

The extension works only with the organization's Acxxel application and relevant
GeM pages. It is intended for authorized internal Bid Analyser users.
