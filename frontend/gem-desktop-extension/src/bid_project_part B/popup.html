Acxxel GeM Checking...
Disqualified Bid Tracking

Log in to GeM and click Scan. The extension opens the Seller Bid List, applies the Technical Evaluated filters and starts reading. Retry resumes directly at the saved page.

Bid To Be Participated

Select one approved GeM category. The scan uses Bid Start Date: Latest First and stops automatically at the first bid older than the current three-day window. Repeat for each category.

Awarded Bid/RA Ranking

Select Bids/RAs Already Submitted/Participated and Bid/RA Awarded. The scan automatically checks pages 1-65 and saves bids with Start Date 01/01/2026 or later.

Product Scan

Search your product on GeM Market (category + filters like Brand), then click Scan. The scan reads the open page and every product on it, saves them, and continues page by page to the last page. Only acxxel products are taken. Desktop, All in One PC and Toner products also appear in the product directory; every category gets its own Excel.
