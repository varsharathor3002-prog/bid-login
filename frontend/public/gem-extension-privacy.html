Acxxel GeM Desktop Workflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This extension helps authorized Acxxel Bid Analyser users transfer approved Desktop catalogue information from the Acxxel application into forms opened by the user on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM).

Data handled

The extension handles an Acxxel authentication token, approved bid and product specifications, catalogue prices and quantities, approved product documents and images, job status, and the contents of relevant GeM form controls. It does not collect GeM passwords, captcha answers, or OTPs.

How data is used

Data is used only to authenticate the authorized employee, retrieve the selected internal job, fill the corresponding GeM catalogue form, upload approved assets, and report workflow progress to Acxxel. Final submission remains a manual user action.

Storage and sharing

The authentication token and active job identifier are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. Workflow data is exchanged only between the extension, the organization's Acxxel service, and the GeM pages on which the user activates the workflow. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Security and retention

Production deployments must use HTTPS. Authentication tokens are time-limited and are removed when invalid or expired. Job and audit data is retained by the organization according to its internal procurement and compliance requirements.

User control

Users manually sign in to GeM, navigate to Add New Offering, review all populated values, may edit any value, and manually perform final submission. Removing the extension deletes its local workflow access.

Limited use

Use of information received through this extension is limited to providing and improving its single procurement-workflow purpose. The data is not used for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling, and is not made available for humans to read except where required for user-requested support, security, legal compliance, or internal procurement operations.
