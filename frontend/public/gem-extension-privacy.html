Acxxel GeM Desktop Workflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

The Acxxel GeM Desktop Workflow extension is published by Laps N Tabs Technology Pvt. Ltd. It is an internal business tool used by authorized Acxxel employees to fill approved bid and catalogue details on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM, gem.gov.in) and to read GeM bid results back into the organization's Acxxel bidding application (acxxelbidding.com).

Data handled

  • Authentication information: the Acxxel application login token, so the extension can act for the signed-in employee. The extension does not collect GeM passwords, captcha answers, or OTPs.
  • Approved bid and catalogue data from Acxxel: product specifications, catalogue prices and quantities, approved product documents and images, and job status, used to fill GeM forms.
  • Content of GeM pages the user opens: bid numbers, bid and reverse-auction results, financial rankings, seller participation status, item details, and the buyer department, organisation and consignee address shown on a bid, along with the contents of relevant GeM form controls.

The extension does not collect browsing history, location, keystrokes, personal communications, health information, or payment or card details. It runs only on GeM pages and on the Acxxel application.

How data is used

Data is used only to authenticate the authorized employee, retrieve the selected internal job, fill the corresponding GeM form, upload approved assets, sync GeM bid results, rankings and seller statuses into Acxxel, and report workflow progress. Final submission on GeM remains a manual user action.

Storage and sharing

The authentication token and active job identifier are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. Workflow data is exchanged only between the extension, the organization's Acxxel service at acxxelbidding.com, and the GeM pages on which the user activates the workflow. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Security and retention

Production deployments use HTTPS. Authentication tokens are time-limited and are removed when invalid or expired. Bid, job and audit data is retained by the organization according to its internal procurement and compliance requirements.

User control

Users manually sign in to GeM, review all populated values, may edit any value, and manually perform final submission. Removing the extension deletes its local workflow access.

Limited use

Use of information received through this extension is limited to providing and improving its single procurement-workflow purpose. The data is not used for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling, and is not made available for humans to read except where required for user-requested support, security, legal compliance, or internal procurement operations.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's handling of data changes, this page and its "Last updated" date will be updated.
